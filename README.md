## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions
<a id="faq"></a>
<details>
<summary><b>What is iTunes used for?</b></summary>
<br>
iTunes is used for playing, organizing, and managing multimedia content, including music, videos, and podcasts. It also serves as a store for purchasing media and a tool for syncing content with Apple devices.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is iTunes free to use?</b></summary>
<br>
Yes, iTunes is available as a complete free version with all features included, allowing you to enjoy multimedia content without any limitations.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I use iTunes without an Apple device?</b></summary>
<br>
Absolutely! iTunes can be used on Windows PCs for managing and playing music, movies, and other multimedia content regardless of having an Apple device.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I sync my iPhone with iTunes?</b></summary>
<br>
Connect your iPhone to your PC using a USB cable, open iTunes, and select your device. Follow the on-screen prompts to sync your media library with your iPhone.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Where does iTunes store my music?</b></summary>
<br>
iTunes stores your music in the "iTunes" folder located in your "My Music" directory on your PC, along with media files in the "iTunes Media" folder.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How often is iTunes updated?</b></summary>
<br>
iTunes is regularly updated to improve performance, security, and functionality. It's recommended to check for updates frequently.
</details>
